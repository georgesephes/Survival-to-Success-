SPEAKING & WORKSHOPS

Ideas that invite people to see differently—and move differently.

George facilitates workshops and conversations designed to be practical, reflective, challenging, and human. The goal is not simply to leave people inspired. It is to leave them with something they can use.

FEATURED WORKSHOPS

01

Hurt, Heal, & Grow

A reflective workshop centered on the relationship between pain, healing, personal growth, and what it takes to move forward without allowing past hurt to dictate the future.

02

Breaking the Cycle of Survival

An exploration of how a necessary season of survival can become a repeated way of life—and how people can begin recognizing the patterns, beliefs, behaviors, and conditions keeping them in the cycle.

03

Mission Possible: Living Out Your Life's Mission at Work

A workshop on the relationship between purpose, vocation, identity, and daily work—helping participants think beyond job tasks and consider how their work connects to mission and impact.

04

Building Transformational Spaces

A session for leaders and organizations examining how culture, relationships, systems, and environments can either reinforce survival or create the conditions where people can grow.
